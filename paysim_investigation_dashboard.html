What happens after faithfulness verification?

Automated recovery, evidence recheck, then a separate risk policy decision.

Two decisions · one workflow
01 · VERIFY

Faithfulness verifier

Checks that explanation feature names and stated directions match the transaction’s supplied SHAP evidence.

02 · AI REPAIR

Qwen explanation repair

For flagged cases, Qwen rewrites the note using only the saved SHAP evidence and preserves the model score.

03 · GROUNDED FALLBACK

Evidence-only recovery

When free-form AI text still fails, the system builds a concise note directly from the positive and negative SHAP lists.

04 · RECHECK

Verifier runs again

The repaired note is checked with the same verifier. Unresolved explanations would be sent to the review queue.

05 · PRIORITIZE

Risk policy recommends action

Saved probability, prediction, and threshold determine routine monitoring, added screening, or analyst priority.

What AI does: repairs explanation wording from existing SHAP evidence. What AI does not decide: whether a transaction is fraud or which risk action to take; those are based on the saved model output and explicit rules.
